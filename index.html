Why XTOM exists

We build because real problems can't wait. We share because curiosity should never cost a thing. That's why XTOM exists.

Vision

A world where anyone, anywhere, can understand how technology works and use it to build something of their own.

Mission

Design practical engineering, software and learning tools for real life, and publish what we learn openly, without a paywall.

XTOM-002 · Vision · 02/09

Fields we work in

Seven areas where engineering meets daily life, from the open sea to your desk. Each one is a home for future XTOM projects and free courses.

A

Mechanical design

From first sketch to CAD, tolerances and a part that actually fits.

B

Robotics

Arms, grippers and control systems that take over repetitive or risky work.

C

Aerospace technology

Lighter, stronger airframes and wings shaped by aerodynamics, for aircraft and UAVs.

D

3D printing

Rapid prototypes and printable spare parts that anyone can make at home.

E

Nature-inspired technology

Fins, shells and leaves as blueprints for quieter, more efficient machines.

F

Underwater technology

ROVs, ocean sensors and hull design for working safely below the surface.

G

Advanced ship design

Efficient hulls, wind-assisted propulsion and CFD-driven optimization for cleaner shipping.

XTOM-007 · Open knowledge · 07/09

Meet the founder

I'm Muthair, a mechanical and ocean engineer. I love robotics, aerospace technology, mechanical design, underwater technology, 3D printing and every machine nature has already engineered better than we have.

I started XTOM to turn that curiosity into things people can use: everyday apps, engineering solutions for real problems, and free learning for anyone who wants it. Technology and knowledge shouldn't sit behind a subscription.
